Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Carbon360s is currently under development.

What this covers

This page describes how the Carbon360s website handles information you provide through the contact form. The full platform, marketplace and any user accounts are not yet live.

Information we collect

When you use the contact form we receive the name, email address, phone number (if provided), interest category and message you submit. These are sent to the email address associated with the form (ssaurav9572@gmail.com) so the team can reply.

We do not currently run advertising trackers, analytics pixels or user accounts on this site. Basic server logs from the hosting provider may record IP addresses and browser information for security and operational purposes.

How we use it

We use contact form submissions solely to respond to your inquiry, discuss potential pilot participation, offtake interest, partnerships or general questions. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for marketing.

Third-party form service

The contact form is processed by FormSubmit.co (or a similar service). Their handling of the data is subject to their own privacy practices. We choose a simple service that forwards the message to our email without requiring an account on your side.

Retention

Messages are retained in the team’s email inbox for as long as needed to respond and maintain ordinary business records. You may request deletion of a specific message by replying to any correspondence.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you submitted via the contact form. Contact us at the email above or through the form on the home page.

Changes

As the platform develops we will update this policy and the site to reflect any new data practices (accounts, marketplace transactions, verification data, etc.). Material changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: use the contact form on the home page or email ssaurav9572@gmail.com.
