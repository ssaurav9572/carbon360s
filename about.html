About Carbon360s

Building the missing link between small farms and carbon buyers in eastern India.

What we are building

Carbon360s is a carbon marketplace under development. The goal is simple: make buying and selling verified carbon credits frictionless so that small farmers can participate and earn a share of the proceeds.

We focus on eastern India, starting with Jharkhand. The model combines trusted local onboarding, field mapping, methodology matching, independent verification by accredited agencies, and direct sales to company buyers.

Opportunity snapshot

Large land base, early market. Figures below are from public sources or our planning assumptions and are labelled accordingly.

179.4 million ha

India cultivable land, 2024–25 (provisional). Source references in investor materials.

$30–50 billion

India carbon market potential by 2050 at $15 a credit across agriculture, land restoration and forests (various reports).

₹194

Average Indian credit price per tonne, Q1 2024 (The Quint and related reporting).

Our pilot target

1,000-acre pilot, then 15,000 acres by Year 3. This is an internal target, not a market-size claim.

How we differ

Funded companies exist in soil carbon and removal projects (Varaha, Grow Indigo, Boomitra and others). Most operate at broader scale or with remote-sensing emphasis. Our stated focus is village-level trust and access in eastern India through cooperative channels and local coordinators.

We do not claim exclusivity. We claim a practical combination that has not yet been executed at this level in the region: local relationships + project development + marketplace sales.

Global context

Rules in several jurisdictions are moving toward group certification for small farmers and third-party verification. The EU carbon farming framework is one example. Indian projects do not automatically qualify for EU registries. We treat global developments as signals and possible future routes, not current guarantees.

Expansion path we are planning: Jharkhand → Bihar and Odisha → additional states, plus partner aggregators who bring their own farmers.

Risks we acknowledge

Plots may not qualify

Methodology fit, baseline and additionality can reject land.

Price and yield

Average prices have been modest. Credit yield per acre depends on the methodology and practices. Both are unproven at our scale until verified.

Timeline

No credits before roughly month 18. Independent checks can run longer than planned.

Relationships

Cooperative access is a relationship, not yet a signed agreement. Farmer numbers are not committed.

Current stage

Carbon360s is under development. The landing page, demo marketplace and MRV explanations are previews. Software v1, research dataset, pilot enrolment, validation and first offtake are still ahead. Team is growing toward the milestone plan.
