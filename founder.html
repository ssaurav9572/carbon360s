Our journey

Stock360s first, then Revenue360s — a live business platform that is now being reused as the operational base for new products including Carbon360s. The team understands both technology delivery and the realities of working with Indian businesses and, now, rural networks.

Carbon360s is at seed stage and under active development. The team is growing toward the milestone plan (approximately 12 people by month 7 in the current estimates).
